## Course Throughline

**Communication as Access-to-Agency:** communication, sensory access, AAC, behavior support, family knowledge, identity, and classroom conditions are evidence. Support should make students easier to understand, not easier to control.

The **Puzzle Plan** combines lived experience, education, data, research, technology, and advocacy so that no diagnosis or data point stands alone. **EQUITAS** is its equity-centered heart: whose knowledge is included, what context is missing, how power and bias shape interpretation, and whether support increases dignity, agency, belonging, and access.
