## Final Review Questions

- Does the support increase agency, not only reduce behavior?
- Is every behavior description observable rather than moralized?
- Are unknowns marked honestly?
- Are student, family, and lived-experience sources treated as evidence?
- Are communication modes and participation routes treated equitably?
- Does the plan distinguish immediate safety from neurodivergent difference?
- Does data include the environment and adult implementation?
- Is every source named accurately?
