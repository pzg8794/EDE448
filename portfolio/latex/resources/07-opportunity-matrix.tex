\toolsection{What This Resource Is}

A communication opportunity matrix starts with ordinary routines and specifies what the learner may need to communicate, what partners must do, and what evidence would show that access is real. It prevents AAC or other communication support from appearing only during a specialist session or adult-led trial. General education becomes a rich communicative environment when academic, social, choice-making, refusal, and repair opportunities are embedded throughout the day \citep{downing2015classroom}.

The companion partner protocol matters because vocabulary availability is not enough. A message must be noticed, interpreted collaboratively, and allowed to affect the interaction. Functional communication that is repeatedly acknowledged but not honored becomes an unreliable pathway \citep{peckhamhardin2015behavior}.

\toolsection{Seven-Move Partner Protocol}

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Position access:} Keep the primary system and backup within reach and view.
  \item \textbf{Model, do not test:} Use relevant language on a partner board without demanding imitation.
  \item \textbf{Ask once and wait:} Leave at least ten seconds when that pace fits the learner.
  \item \textbf{Notice every mode:} Gesture, movement, expression, objects, photos, writing, speech, and AAC count.
  \item \textbf{Confirm without narrowing:} State what you noticed and offer choices plus ``something else.''
  \item \textbf{Honor the message:} When safe, let refusal, correction, request, or question change what happens.
  \item \textbf{Repair together:} If meaning remains unclear, pause and try another route instead of escalating prompts.
\end{enumerate}

Useful partner language includes: ``I saw you move the sample away. Are you saying \textit{stop}, \textit{different tool}, \textit{need space}, or something else?'' and ``You told me \textit{not that}. Show me another way when you are ready.'' Avoid physical prompting without consent, taking a device as a consequence, speaking about the learner as if absent, or asking for device repetition after a clear message in another mode.

\reflectionbox{The four-pathway display was built for EDU486 after access planning had to survive microscopes, samples, movement, model revision, policy talk, and a public showcase \citep{campEvidence2026}. It kept the rigorous goal in the center while making non-contact observation, camera evidence, home language, quiet partnering, low-energy roles, and re-entry visible. The AAC profile remains prospective; success is whether a learner can author evidence, set boundaries, repair meaning, and alter group decisions, not the number of device activations.}

\adaptationbox{Confirm the matrix with the actual learner and family, including language, motor, sensory, literacy, cultural, and privacy needs. Remove opportunities that feel intrusive and add messages the learner identifies. Monitor partner behavior as closely as student behavior. If prompts rise while authorship falls, or messages do not change outcomes, revise the opportunity or partner response.}
