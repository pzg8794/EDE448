\toolsection{What This Resource Is}

This record replaces adult-centered ratings with a two-way account of communication, access conditions, barriers, and student-authored meaning. The Daily Communication Logs packet includes useful home--school structures, but several examples emphasize good/fair/poor days, warnings, sitting, compliance, or initials \citep{dailylogs2009}. Those fields can make the student an object of reporting and hide whether the environment, support, or adult response contributed to the day.

The redesigned record supports communication and behavior by preserving what the student chose, refused, asked, commented, or repaired; how the message was expressed; which support increased access; and what barrier should change. Family context travels back to school as evidence rather than as an afterthought. The learner reviews, changes, or declines the outgoing message.

\toolsection{Reverse-Engineered Practice Artifact: July 29 Two-Way Record}

The camp team produced a real family update, photo story, print PDF, and email grounded in transcripts and reviewed photographs. The completed half below preserves that package's sequence and language while omitting participant names, faces, and private contact information. The return half simulates what families \emph{could} fill out in a future two-way version; it is intentionally blank because no completed parent form was recovered. This is a reconstruction from authentic materials, not a claim that the exact form was sent.

\toolsection{Use and Consent Protocol}

Agree on purpose, language, frequency, and readers; let the student select, edit, approve, or withhold what travels; offer speech, AAC, writing, drawing, photographs, objects, partner-assisted selection, or no message; and invite a family response by paper, phone, digital message, translation, or a less-frequent schedule. Describe observable conditions rather than assigning moral ratings. Review whether an entry leads adults to change a recurring barrier.

Family knowledge is evidence, not an afterthought \citep{zacarian2015community}. Absence of a reply is not lack of care, and a family should not have to disclose diagnosis, health, trauma, or private circumstances to request a usable support.

\reflectionbox{The authentic camp package centered work, learning, youth words, participation routes, significance, and an evidence limit rather than a good/fair/poor rating \citep{campEvidence2026}. Its limitation was that it remained a group-level message from adults to families. This redesign preserves that evidence-rich architecture while adding student approval and a low-burden family return path, so the school narrative is not the only account that travels.}

\adaptationbox{Daily reporting can become surveillance, expose private health/disability information, or create family burden. Collect only information needed for support; follow school privacy policy; avoid public or unsecured channels; and honor the student's right not to share. Some learners may prefer a weekly summary, event-triggered message, photo choice, or no formal log. The form changes when consent or usefulness changes.}
