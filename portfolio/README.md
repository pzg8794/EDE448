## Completed Communication System

The portfolio's evidence core is the communication system already built during EDU486, not a set of hypothetical supports:

| Timescale | Completed product | Communication and support function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| After each activity | 30-Second Student Video Memo | Preserves what the learner did, experienced, learned or confirmed, and wants investigated next |
| Every day | Every-Day Personalized Science Souvenir | Returns a student-centered learning portrait with evidence, significance, and uncertainty |
| End of day | Day Recap Evidence Story | Reconstructs every activity for youth review, next-day redesign, collaborators, and family communication |
| End of week | Full-Week Personalized Family Recap | Gives each attended day its own page and each day five activity cards; completed packages are documented as prepared, not verified as delivered |

Teaching-placement learning supplied concrete access and classroom-system knowledge. The Puzzle Plan keeps student voice, artifacts, context, and adult interpretation from collapsing into one data point; EQUITAS tests whose account shapes the next lesson, institutional understanding, and family narrative.

## Assignment Core

Create a practical, individualized collection of strategies and resources for a future classroom or professional setting. The portfolio should support students with autism and other complex support needs, with particular attention to communication, social interaction, and behavior as communication.

The Blackboard prompt contains the internally inconsistent phrase "at least ten (8) resources/supports." This repository uses the more demanding interpretation: **10 resources/supports**, each developed in **1-2 pages**.

Every resource must clearly explain:

- the strategy or resource for a teacher/family audience
- how it supports communication, social-emotional learning, or behavior
- when and why it would be used in practice
- relevant sources, references, or websites

The prompt encourages artifacts from practice and reuse of at least five applicable module assignments. Appropriate areas include AAC, social-emotional supports, inclusive classroom resources, autism-inclusion lessons, positive and proactive behavior supports, classroom systems and routines, restorative or relationship-based approaches, and communication support plans.

## Rubric

The 40-point rubric assigns 10 points to each area:

| Criterion | Exemplary Standard Used Here |
| --- | --- |
| Scholarly connections to resources | Current, authoritative sources directly support claims; the text explains connections to strategies and policy or professional guidance; citations use a recognized format. |
| Organization and clarity of resources | Resources are consistently labeled, categorized, easy to locate, and strengthened by useful visuals or annotations. |
| Relevance to classroom practice | Every resource includes concrete use cases showing how it informs instruction, student outcomes, and behavior support. |
| Evidence and reflection depth | Reflections explain how evidence changed planning, acknowledge limitations, and identify student-responsive adaptations. |

## Submission Standard

The final portfolio uses **Communication as Access-to-Agency** as its organizing theme. Each resource is independently usable, public-safe, strengths-based, and explicit about dignity, consent, neurodiversity, student voice, implementation, adaptation, and evidence limits.
