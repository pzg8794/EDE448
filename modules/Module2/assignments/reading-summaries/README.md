## Source Corrections Recorded

- The local `Dunlap PBS and Autism.pdf` is a 2012 PBS chapter, not the required 2019 PTR book.
- The ProQuest book-details PDF is for Paula Kluth's 2023 third edition, not PTR.
- The local `Goldstein` filenames identify Phyllis Kaufman **Goodstein** in the source text.
- The local `Zacaria (2016)` filename contains a spelling/year mismatch; the source is Zacarian and Silverstone (2015).
- The local `Jorgenseon` filename is a typo; the author is Cheryl M. Jorgensen.

## Use Standard

Before retaining a source in a final submission:

- confirm that Piter meaningfully engaged with it
- trace each claim to that source
- use accurate author, year, title, and edition metadata
- distinguish a full reading from an access or table-of-contents record
- evaluate content through dignity, consent, neurodiversity, and EQUITAS
